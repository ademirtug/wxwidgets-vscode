# Third-party artwork

The extension icon (`assets/icon.svg` and `assets/icon.png`) adapts the three-block motif of the [wxWidgets logo](https://wxwidgets.org/downloads/logos/), published by the wxWidgets project under the [Creative Commons ShareAlike 1.0 Generic license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/sa/1.0/).

This adaptation replaces the original gradients with flat outlined blocks and changes the colors, spacing, and proportions. The adapted icon is distributed under the same CC SA 1.0 license. This artwork license applies to the icon files only.

The icon identifies an independent extension and does not imply affiliation with or endorsement by the wxWidgets project.
